## Wes Shelton

**AI Enablement and Adoption | HR and Benefits Technology | Process Improvement**

I help teams understand their workflows, choose practical uses for AI and automation, and support the people using them. My experience connects HR and benefits operations with IT project delivery, Microsoft 365, and user enablement.

Before moving into IT project delivery in December 2024, I worked in benefits operations at Wayne County, Rocket, and WACKER. Before that I spent four years in customer service at Blue Cross Blue Shield of Michigan. That background shapes how I approach AI adoption: start with the process and the people doing the work, then pick the tool.

I'm open to roles in AI enablement, HR and benefits technology, and benefits operations.

---

### What I work on

- **Enablement and training.** Training outlines, exercises, communications, and user support for people learning new tools, including Microsoft Copilot.
- **Workflow automation.** Power Automate flows and Copilot Studio solutions, built from gathered requirements and delivered with testing plans and user guidance.
- **Process documentation.** Process libraries, swimlane diagrams, and Visio maps that make responsibilities and handoffs clear.
- **Benefits operations.** Enrollment, eligibility, EDI 834 file processing, audits, vendor coordination, and employee education.
